1. LOGO PRINCIPAL — BLASON

ENGLISH

1. PRIMARY LOGO — SHIELD
Use: primary website identity and the wearer’s left side on clothing.
File: MHSV_Logo_Principal_Blason_Approuve.png

2. FOCUS BRAND MARK — CIRCLE WITH CENTRAL DOT
Use: collection brand; the wearer’s right side on clothing and the right side of equipment.
Use the navy-and-gold version on light backgrounds and the white-and-gold version on dark backgrounds.
Files: SVG and 2048 px PNG.

3. INSTITUTIONAL LOGO — THREE FIGURES, STAR AND ARROW
Use: events, official documents, presentations and institutional sections of the website.
Do not place this logo on collection clothing.
The 2400 px PNG files are suitable for the website and presentations. For very large-format printing, request the original source vector file.

4. RULES
Do not use previous variants containing the wording “Beyond Football”.
Do not distort, recolour or reconstruct the logos.
Do not add third-party, sponsor or sportswear-manufacturer branding.
The collection boards are gallery mock-ups. For manufacturing, apply the official SVG files directly to the products.
